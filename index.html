ACL 2026 STUDENT RESEARCH WORKSHOP

Validator-Guided Hard Negative Mining for Masked Language Modeling in Low-Resource Ancient Languages

Sumerian is about as low-resource as language modeling gets: a language isolate with no living relatives to transfer from, and of CDLI's 143,000+ digitized tablets, only 370 carry the part-of-speech annotations needed to train on. This paper asks how far explicit linguistic structure can substitute for scale — a hierarchical validator distilled from those 370 tablets constrains a fine-tuned mBERT's candidate space and supplies hard negatives during training, rather than relying on cross-lingual transfer that isn't available here.

The combination pushes masked-token prediction from an 18.0% baseline to 86.7% hit@10. Testing on tablets from 600 years earlier shows the validator's rules are period-specific and degrade out-of-domain, while BERT's learned representations transfer far more robustly across time — a concrete look at where rule-based and neural components each pull their weight in an extreme low-resource setting.
